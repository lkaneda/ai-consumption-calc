Methodology

Gemini directly measured

// Elsworth et al. (2025) — Google production instrumentation Energy = 0.24 Wh × (output_tokens / 300) Water = 0.26 mL × (output_tokens / 300) // Scaled from fleet median (~300 output tokens). No variant breakdown published.

Claude / ChatGPT

Energy floor Oviedo et al. 2026

// Production-scale H100, continuous batching. Joule peer-reviewed. E_floor = 0.34 Wh × (output_tokens / 300) // Corroborated by Sam Altman disclosure, June 2025

Energy ceiling Jegham et al. 2025

// API latency + TPS benchmarks. Flagship per provider: // Claude → Claude 3.7 Sonnet (j_s 0.950 Wh) | ChatGPT → GPT-4o (j_s 0.423 Wh) // Oviedo argues this overstates production energy by 4–20× // Current models (Claude 4.x, GPT-5) not yet in peer-reviewed literature E_ceil = j_s × (output_tokens / 300) // j_s = short-query (~300 token) measured value, used as rate for session-level scaling

Water derivation Jegham et al. 2025 Eq. 4

Water (mL) = [(E_kwh / PUE) × WUE_site + E_kwh × WUE_source] × 1000 // WUE_site: AWS 0.12 L/kWh (Amazon, 2025); Azure 0.27 L/kWh (Microsoft, 2026)
Sources

Gemini (directly measured): Elsworth et al. (2025) "Measuring the environmental impact of delivering AI at Google Scale." arXiv:2508.15734. The only directly measured, published, production-scale figure for any major AI assistant. Fleet-wide — no variant breakdown available.

Claude / ChatGPT energy floor: Oviedo et al. (2026) "Energy Use of AI Inference: Efficiency Pathways and Test-Time Scaling." Joule. DOI: 10.1016/j.joule.2026.102430. Frontier model production median 0.34 Wh per 300 output tokens. Corroborated by Sam Altman, June 2025.

Claude ceiling — Claude 3.7 Sonnet: Jegham et al. (2025) "How Hungry is AI?" arXiv:2505.09598v6 Table 4. Short: 0.950 Wh | Medium: 2.989 Wh | Long: 5.671 Wh.

ChatGPT ceiling — GPT-4o: Jegham et al. (2025) Table 4. Short: 0.423 Wh | Medium: 1.215 Wh | Long: 2.875 Wh.

Water formula + WUE/PUE: Formula from Jegham et al. (2025) Eq. 4 + Table 1. PUE unchanged: AWS 1.14, Azure 1.12 (no updated disclosures). WUE_source unchanged: AWS 5.11, Azure 4.35 (Jegham et al. Table 1). WUE_site updated: AWS (Claude) 0.12 L/kWh per Amazon data center sustainability disclosure (aboutamazon.com, 2025); Azure (ChatGPT) 0.27 L/kWh per Microsoft Environmental blog (blogs.microsoft.com, June 2026).

AWS WUE_site (Claude): Amazon (2025) "Amazon data center water usage." aboutamazon.com/news/sustainability/amazon-data-center-water-usage. Self-reported 0.12 L/kWh in 2025, a 52% improvement since 2021.

Azure WUE_site (ChatGPT): Microsoft (2026) "Inside Microsoft's two-decade push to cut water intensity while scaling for growth." blogs.microsoft.com, June 24 2026. Self-reported 0.27 L/kWh for owned datacenter fleet in 2025.

Data gap: Published benchmarks cover models through late 2025. Current models (Claude 4.x, GPT-5, Gemini 2.x) not yet independently measured. Efficiency trends suggest actual figures are lower.
